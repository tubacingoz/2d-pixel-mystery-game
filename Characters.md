# Character Profiles & Relationships

This document outlines the backstories, motivations, and player-interaction mechanics of the main characters (NPCs) in the [Game Name] universe.

**Protagonist (Player Character)**
* **Role:** The newcomer who arrives in town and takes over a decaying farm.
* **Motivation:** Restoring the farm during the day while unraveling the town's underlying mystery at night.
* **Mechanic Impact:** An energy bar restricts day/night activities. The character's dialogue choices determine the Trust Level of the townsfolk.

**[NPC Name] - The Suspicious Botanist**
* **Role:** The town's plant expert; possesses secret knowledge about the strange nocturnal events.
* **Personality:** Aloof, analytical, and secretive.
* **Story Arc:** Initially distrusts the player. As the Trust Level increases, they provide special crafting recipes that help solve the mysteries.

**[NPC Name] - The Mayor**
* **Role:** The town's extroverted, charismatic, yet overly defensive leader.
* **Personality:** Highly welcoming on the surface, but constantly trying to cover things up in the background.
* **Story Arc:** The main figure actively trying to obstruct the player's night-time investigations. 

**[NPC Name] - The Night Watchman / Merchant**
* **Role:** A character who only appears during the night cycle, selling black market or mysterious items.
* **Personality:** Eccentric, opportunistic, and dangerous.
* **Story Arc:** Sells clues to the player, but there is a risk that some of the information provided may be false (deception mechanics).
